Sales playbook · based on your uploaded call script

1 · Opening and permissionIdentify yourself as the AI assistant for Fayzora Technologies. Ask if it is a convenient time. If busy, offer a callback.
2 · DiscoveryAsk one question at a time: website/app status, how customers find them, manual workflows, and the most useful digital improvement.
3 · Match the service to the needWebsite development, Android/iOS apps, custom software for billing/inventory/CRM, banner and graphic design, SEO, social media, and maintenance.
4 · ObjectionsToo expensive → offer a tailored quote or phased scope. Not now → offer materials only if welcome. Already have a developer → offer complementary support. Needs to think → offer a proposal or demo.
5 · Close with a next stepOffer a free 15-minute consultation, subject to availability. Confirm follow-up channel and timing; never claim a message or booking succeeded unless an integration confirms it.
6 · Outcome and respectRecord CONVERTED, NURTURE, or DEAD LEAD. Honor “do not call” immediately. Never invent pricing, testimonials, results, or website details.

Available in this dashboard

Integrations to configure

  • Google Maps / business discovery: approved data provider and API key
  • Company websites: crawler/search provider with rate and privacy controls
  • Existing CRM: API base URL, authentication, and field mapping
  • Real calling: eligible SIP plan, outbound trunk, caller ID, wallet/minutes, E.164 phone numbers, and consent checks

No API keys are requested or stored in this demo UI.
